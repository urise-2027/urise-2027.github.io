Featured Talk · Filming Cultural Memory: Indigenous Narratives, Identity, and the Politics of Representation
Khalid Hasan Khan · New York Film Academy-trained producer and filmmaker · 26+ international awards · 100+ festival screenings
View biography

Khalid Hasan Khan is a producing graduate of New York Film Academy, Los Angeles, United States, and an award-winning filmmaker with over 26 international awards and more than 100 international film-festival screenings worldwide. Known for his concept-driven storytelling and genre-defying approach, Khan’s work spans psychological thrillers, social documentaries, environmental filmmaking and organic narratives. His cinematic vision bridges classical storytelling traditions with emerging technologies, creating films that are intellectually engaging, socially relevant and globally resonant. His diverse filmography includes Hotal, his double award-winning debut psychological thriller; The Unmasking, exploring vaccine hesitancy; The Painterist, examining intellectual piracy; Twinkling Without Shining, documenting the lives of Afghan refugee children; Barefoot, an interfaith sports documentary; Murder of Mystic, an environmental documentary; and Time Sentence, an AI-driven courtroom drama. From human-interest documentaries and socially conscious cinema to literary mysteries and psychological dramas, Khan continues to explore the boundaries of cinematic expression. His work is united by an enduring commitment to originality, experimentation and stories that transcend geographical and cultural boundaries. His recent works include Screen Locked, an award-winning techno-thriller on digital addiction; Vellichor, an upcoming literary thriller; and Fury on the Shortwave, a forthcoming anti-war thriller.
